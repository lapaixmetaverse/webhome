— PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE / EFFECTIVE 15 JULY 2026

Privacy by design.

Sacred Word is built to keep your spiritual practice close to you. This policy explains the small amount of information involved in operating optional account and context features.

ON THIS PAGEWhat we processSpeech dataLocal storageIntegrationsCookiesYour rights

1. What we process

We process only what is needed to operate Sacred Word. Depending on your chosen features, this can include an email address or authentication identifier, account metadata, device-generated progress records, and support messages you send us. We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from your Scripture reading.

2. Speech-to-text stays local

When you use the Forge, the browser or app may transcribe your reading to compare it with a displayed verse. Raw voice recordings are never uploaded to Sacred Word. The intended architecture processes speech locally in your browser or app; text alignment and session state are handled on-device unless you deliberately enable a separate sync feature.

Device speech services can be controlled by your operating system or browser. Check their privacy controls before enabling microphone access. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in device or browser settings.

3. Local-first storage and encryption

Progress, locally generated identity material, and session records may be stored in LocalStorage or IndexedDB. The application uses world-class secure encryption to create integrity links for stored records; hashing is not encryption and should not be understood as a password vault. Where encryption is implemented, keys and access controls remain tied to your device context.

Clearing site data removes local records from that browser. We cannot restore erased local data without an independent backup or account-linked service. Never share a mnemonic, recovery phrase, or authentication credential.

4. Limited third-party integrations

Supabase may process authentication metadata such as account identifiers and session tokens when you create or use a cloud account. Gemini API may receive a context-search query where you expressly use an AI-assisted Scripture context feature. Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information in such queries. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

We configure integrations to use the minimum data required for their function. We do not intentionally send raw voice recordings, complete private reading history, or local recovery phrases to these providers.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential local storage or cookies to remember security preferences, session state, and basic display choices. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If optional analytics are introduced, we will provide notice and any consent required by applicable law.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal information. GDPR users may also withdraw consent where processing depends on consent and may complain to a supervisory authority. CCPA/CPRA users may request to know, correct, or delete personal information and will not be discriminated against for exercising rights.

To delete local data, use your browser’s site-data controls. To request deletion of a cloud account or make a privacy request, contact privacy@sacredword.lapaix from the relevant account email. We may verify your request before acting and retain limited records where law requires it.

7. Security, retention, and updates

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. Cloud account metadata is retained only while needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. We may revise this policy as the platform evolves; the effective date above identifies the current version.
